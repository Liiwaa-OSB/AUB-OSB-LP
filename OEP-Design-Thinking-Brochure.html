Business-Centric Design Thinking

The Innovate to Elevate Program explores the requirements needed for enabling customer-focused innovation that is key to facing increasingly competitive environments. It focuses on enabling teams to turn innovative ideas into execution through design thinking. Participants will have the opportunity to understand the principles of design thinking and apply them in team-based activities.
