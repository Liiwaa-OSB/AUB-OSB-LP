Pricing Analytics
Program Brochure

Pricing is one of the most powerful levers a company can use to drive profitability and competitiveness, yet it remains a complex challenge that businesses face if reduced to cost plus- or competition-based approaches. This program focuses on pricing as a competitive driver, balancing the tradeoff between faster resource/inventory turnover and higher margins through data analytics capabilities.
