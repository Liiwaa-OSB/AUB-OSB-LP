AI in Supply Chain
Program Brochure

Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping supply chain management by boosting efficiency, enhancing forecasting accuracy, enabling smarter decision-making, and strengthening operational resilience. The AI in Supply Chain Program focuses on real-world AI applications through a combination of executive-level lectures and guided, non-technical hands‑on activities that allow participants to develop practical literacy in how AI-driven systems shape modern supply chains.
