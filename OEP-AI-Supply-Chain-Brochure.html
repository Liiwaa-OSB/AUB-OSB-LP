AI in Supply Chain

The AI in Supply Chain Program focuses on real-world AI applications in production, demand forecasting, inventory management, logistics optimization, and risk mitigation. Participants will develop practical literacy in how AI-driven systems shape modern supply chains and how to interpret their outputs to make informed strategic decisions.
