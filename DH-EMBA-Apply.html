Why Choose OSB's Executive MBA?

OSB's Executive MBA equips accomplished professionals with the strategic mindset, leadership capabilities, and global perspective needed to lead transformation and make enterprise-wide decisions. For over 25 years, the program has developed leaders across the MENA region. Participants learn from internationally recognized faculty, collaborate with a diverse cohort of experienced professionals, benefit from personalized executive coaching, and broaden their global perspective through an international residency creating a transformative learning experience that extends beyond the classroom.

An Investment in Lifelong Leadership

The OSB Executive MBA is more than a degree. It's an investment in your future as a leader. Through an integrated curriculum covering strategy, leadership, AI and digital transformation, innovation, finance, marketing, and operations, you will develop the knowledge, analytical skills, and leadership capabilities to lead organizations with confidence.

Your learning is reinforced through personalized executive coaching and culminates in a Capstone Project, where you apply your knowledge to solve a real business challenge, often within your own organization, transforming classroom insights into practical solutions that deliver immediate value and measurable impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should apply?

The OSB Executive MBA is designed for experienced professionals, senior managers, directors, entrepreneurs, and business leaders who are ready to broaden their strategic perspective, strengthen their leadership capabilities, and accelerate their career growth. Applicants are typically expected to have a minimum of eight years of professional experience, with significant managerial or leadership responsibilities.

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes. The OSB Executive MBA is specifically designed for working professionals. Its flexible learning format, combined with 9 residencies, allows participants to continue advancing their careers while pursuing their degree, minimizing disruption to their professional and personal commitments.

What is the minimum work experience required?

Applicants should have at least eight years of professional work experience, including substantial managerial or leadership responsibilities. The program attracts a diverse cohort of executives with an average of 12 years of professional experience, creating a rich peer-learning environment.

When does the next cohort begin?

The OSB Executive MBA welcomes one cohort each year, beginning in September. We encourage prospective participants to contact the admissions team early to learn about application deadlines, admissions events, and upcoming information sessions.

Is the international residency included?

Yes. The program includes an international residency at a partner global business school, providing participants with valuable international exposure, opportunities to engage with global faculty and business leaders, and insights into international business practices. The international residency is an integral part of the OSB Executive MBA learning experience.
